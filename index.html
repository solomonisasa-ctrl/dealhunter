    Alerts

    When he holds Help, the Alexas you choose announce it, and family phones get an urgent notification with an I'm on my way button. Other messages reach family phones only when he taps Send to phone.

    Pocket fall button (Flic)

    In the Flic app, set the button's Hold action to Internet Request, method GET, and paste this address. Holding the button then alerts family phones right away, and this tablet adds the Alexa announcement.

    1. On each family phone, install the free ntfy app and subscribe to (urgent help) and (messages he sends with Send to phone).
    2. To message him, publish to in the ntfy app. It pops up on his tablet and is read out loud.
    3. Keep a wireless caregiver pager by his chair too, in case the internet is down.

    Tablet setup

    1. Open this page in Chrome, then tap the menu and choose Add to Home screen. Open it from that icon so it fills the screen.
    2. Turn on App pinning (Settings, Security, Other security settings, Pin app). Pin this app so he can't leave it by accident.
    3. Set Screen timeout to the longest option and keep the tablet plugged in. The page also asks the tablet to keep the screen on.
    4. Turn media volume all the way up, or pair a small Bluetooth speaker so he can be heard over the TV.
